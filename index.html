Summary

A spatial and single-cell multi-omics atlas of Lamin A/C-related dilated cardiomyopathy. Single-nucleus profiles from Stanford LMNA-DCM hearts are harmonised with published human and mouse laminopathy cohorts, and paired with Visium sections so a cell state defined in dissociated data can be located back in intact myocardium.

Zonal map of injury
Spatial transcriptomic mapping delineates zonal tiers of fibrosis and DNA-damage burden across the myocardial wall.
Cross-species harmonisation
Human and mouse laminopathy nuclei integrated under one annotation, with shared stress-state labels.
Mechanosensitive rupture
LMNA mutant iPSC-CMs uniquely show nuclear blebbing, γH2AX accumulation and apoptosis under mechanical stress, rescued by blebbistatin.
PARP1–NAD+ axis
PARP1 hyperactivation depletes NAD+ as damage progresses, nominating PARP inhibition and NAD+ repletion.

About

Spatial transcriptomics highlights regional DNA damage in LMNA-linked dilated cardiomyopathy

Siyeon Rhee, Sangkyun Cho, …, Joseph C. Wu · Unpublished, 2025

Visium spatial profiling is combined with joint single-nucleus RNA/ATAC sequencing to chart myocardial damage tiers where cardiomyocyte stress trajectories, DNA damage response and ECM remodelling rise in concert. Patient-derived LMNA iPSC-CMs reveal a mechanosensitive loop — nuclear blebbing, rupture and increasing γH2AX foci — reversible with blebbistatin unloading. The same trajectory exhibits PARP1 hyperactivation and NAD+ depletion, pointing to mechano-metabolic interventions.
